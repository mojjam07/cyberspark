Information we collect. When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you enroll in a paid course, we store the order amount, payment method, and status. We do not store your card details — those are handled directly by Paystack, our payment processor.

How we use it. Your information is used to give you access to purchased courses, send account and payment-related emails (welcome, enrollment confirmation, payment confirmation, password resets), and respond to messages you send us.

Data retention. We retain account and order information for as long as your account is active, and as needed to comply with legal and tax obligations.

Your rights. You can update your profile information at any time from your Account page. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us via our Contact page.

Cookies. We use a session cookie to keep you logged in and a CSRF cookie for security. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

Contact. Questions about this policy? Reach us via our Contact page.
