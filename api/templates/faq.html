Frequently Asked Questions

How can we help?

How do I pay for a course?
At checkout you can choose to pay instantly by card via Paystack, or by direct bank transfer — upload your proof of payment and we'll confirm your enrollment within 24 hours.
How long do I have access to a course?
Every paid course comes with lifetime access — learn at your own pace, revisit lessons whenever you like.
Can I get a refund?
Yes — see our Terms of Service for the full refund policy. In short: full refund within 7 days if you've completed less than 20% of the course.
I paid by bank transfer — why isn't my course showing yet?
Bank transfers are reviewed manually and confirmed within 24 hours. Check your Orders page for status, or contact us if it's been longer.
Do I get a certificate?
Yes — every course includes a certificate of completion once you finish all lessons.
I forgot my password — what do I do?
Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page to reset it by email.
Still have questions?
Reach out to us — we read every message.
